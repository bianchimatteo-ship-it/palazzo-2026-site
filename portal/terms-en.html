2. What POLITICANDO 2026 is

It is a political-simulation game. Games, in-game polls, votes, news, offices won and everything that happens after the start are simulated: they are not electoral information, not forecasts, not political or legal advice. Real data (members of parliament, groups, government, laws, published polls) is shown with its source and kept distinct from what is simulated.
