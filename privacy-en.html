Privacy notice

Version of 22 October 2026 · pursuant to Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). The Italian text is the original; this English text has the same meaning.

In short. You can play without an account: in that case no game data reaches our servers. With an account we keep online your username, a fingerprint of your password (not the password) and the careers you choose to save. There are no ads, no analytics, no profiling and no cookies. You can delete the account at any time.

1. Controller and contacts

The controller is Matteo Bianchi, a natural person. POLITICANDO 2026 is a free game and is not run by a company.
For any privacy or support request: politicando2026simulazione@gmail.com

2. What happens if you play without an account

You can play without creating an account. In that case no game data is sent to our servers: games, settings and start-up reminders stay in the memory of the browser (or of the app) on your device, and are erased by clearing the site data or by deleting the saves from the “Load game” menu. The controller has no access to these games. The same holds for the Android app, which contains the same game: it has no ads, no analytics tools and no third-party SDKs, and it asks for no device permissions (not even location, contacts, microphone, camera or photos: logos are chosen with the system file picker).

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Account, sessions, saves and online logos: providing the service you asked for by creating the account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Email address (optional): verifying the address and recovering the password, at your choice (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • IP address in sign-in attempts: security of the service and prevention of abuse, legitimate interest of the controller (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Technical connection data processed by the infrastructure provider to serve the site: security and operation of the service (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Providing the account data is optional: without an account the game works on your device, without online saves. There is no automated decision-making that produces effects on you.

5. Who receives the data

The site, the account archive and the party logos are hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure (Workers, D1, KV, R2), which processes data on behalf of the controller as processor, under the data processing terms that Cloudflare publishes for its customers. Cloudflare may also process data outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the United States: transfers rely on the safeguards provided by those terms (the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). Cloudflare also processes the technical connection data needed to serve the site.

To send the verification and recovery messages (only if you entered an email address) the service relies on Google (Google Apps Script and Gmail): Google receives the recipient’s address and the text of the message, with the single-use link. Google may also process data outside the European Economic Area, under the terms it publishes for its services. The data is not sold or passed to third parties for advertising or profiling. There are no recipients other than Cloudflare and, for email messages only, Google.

Whoever installs the app from Google Play deals with Google about the installation under the Google Play terms, of which Google is an independent controller; the controller of POLITICANDO 2026 receives from Google Play no personal data of individual users beyond the aggregate data that the console makes available to developers.

The game uses no cookies (it sets none: credentials travel in a header, never in a cookie), has no advertising, no payments, no third-party analytics or profiling tools, and loads no fonts, scripts or other resources from external servers. Links to official sources open the external site only when you tap them.

The only exception is importing a logo from an address, which you start yourself from the “Party logos” section: the browser requests the image, once, from the site that hosts it, which therefore sees your IP address and browser type; it does not know which page you come from, because the game sends no referrer. The image is copied to your device and the address is neither kept nor requested again. This does not happen with logos uploaded as files or with those built in the game.

6. Email and password recovery

If you add an email address to your account, the service uses it for two purposes only: verifying that the address is yours and letting you recover the password. Messages are sent through Google Apps Script and Gmail: Google receives the recipient’s address and the text of the message (which contains a single-use link, valid 48 hours for verification and 30 minutes for recovery). The password is never sent, by email or to anyone else: from the recovery link you choose a new password and the browser derives its fingerprint as at sign-in. Whoever asks for recovery always receives the same answer, whether the account exists or not, and changing the password closes all open sessions. Without a verified address the password cannot be recovered (games saved on your device remain available, and you can export your careers to a file). The sending service is not used for other messages, except the possible warning before deleting inactive accounts, not active today (see “How long we keep data”).

7. Device storage (localStorage, IndexedDB, cache)

The game writes to your device, only to work: games and their backups (localStorage and, when they are large, IndexedDB), settings (language included), interface state, start-up and guide reminders, your personal logo catalog (the images you choose as files or import from an address, cropped, resized and cleaned of their background on the device, tied to your account on this device, copied into IndexedDB, which stays the copy that works offline, and never put into the saves; without an account they never leave the device, with an account they are also copied online as described in section 3, and only after you chose or confirmed the logo; logos added before signing in or with an earlier version are never assigned to the account on their own: we offer them and copy them online only if you choose to) and, if you have an account, the username and the session code. When you sign out, careers stay on the device and online, but are not shown or synchronised until you sign in again with the same account; the logos in one account’s catalog do not appear for another. In the Android app the system may include the app data (games, settings, logos) in the backup of your Google account, if you have it enabled: Android manages it on your device and your account, not the controller. The service worker keeps the game files in the browser cache so that it works offline. These tools are necessary for the service you requested: they are not used to track you or for advertising and are not sent to third parties.

8. How long we keep data

Account data stays until you delete the account. You can delete it at any time (see below): the account, online saves (with previous versions), email address, verification links and sessions are removed together and immediately. Sessions expire after 30 days anyway and, once expired, are deleted by the system: at the first sign-in, registration or deletion request by any user and by an automatic cleanup every day.

At the moment accounts are not deleted for inactivity: they stay until you delete them. If accounts unused for 12 months are deleted in the future, this notice will be updated and holders of a verified email address will receive 30 days’ notice.

9. Your rights and how to exercise them

You have the right to access your data, to have it rectified or erased, to restrict its processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interest and to receive the data in a structured format (Arts. 15–22 GDPR), and to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

  • Deletion: from the game (Menu → Account → “Delete my account”) or from the public page Delete your account, without installing anything. The account password is required.
  • A copy of your data: every career can be exported to a file from the “Load game” menu.
  • Other requests (access, rectification, restriction, objection, an account whose password you do not remember): write to politicando2026simulazione@gmail.com.

10. Children

The game is designed to be usable also by people under 14, without an account: in that case no game data reaches our servers (see section 2), there are no ads, analytics, profiling, purchases or cookies, and no personal data is requested.

The online account, instead, is reserved for people aged at least 14. In the registration form you choose between “under 14” and “14 or older”: the answer only serves to decide whether the account is created, it is neither saved nor sent and it is not an age verification (the controller cannot verify it and asks for no documents). For people under 14 no parental-consent procedure is currently provided: the account is not created and the game is played on the device. The game does not ask for the user’s date of birth: the one shown when creating the character is invented game data and does not concern the player.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe an account was created by a minor without your consent, or you want to ask for the deletion of your child’s data, write to the contact above giving the username: the request is assessed case by case and the account can be deleted. If the minor remembers the password they can delete the account themselves, from the game or from the page Delete your account; if they have a verified email address they can first choose a new one with password recovery.

11. Real people in the game

Real members of parliament, ministers and officials appear with the public data of the institutional sources indicated next to each profile (name, Chamber, group, constituency, offices). Everything that happens in the game — relationships, votes, statements, news, polls — is simulated, and the game does not attribute invented statements or behaviour to real people. To report an error or ask for a correction, write to the controller’s contact.

12. Security and changes

Communications with the service take place over HTTPS; the password is never sent or stored in clear. This notice may be updated: the date at the top indicates the version in force and every substantial change will be indicated here.
